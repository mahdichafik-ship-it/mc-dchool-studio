---
name: Gallery star selection
description: Defines how desktop ratings control family-gallery visibility without changing full studio backup.
---

Every JPEG and RAW capture must continue uploading to the configured studio backup destinations. A family gallery may display, serve, or accept orders for only JPEG captures with a positive star rating. Group JPEGs use the same star rule before being projected to photographed group members.

For automated product preparation, use only exact 5-star source images. Keep one 5-star portrait per project/student and one 5-star class/group image per project/group. Selecting a new 5-star image clears the prior choice to 0 (unrated), not 4; ratings 0–4 may repeat. Portrait and group choices remain separate.

**Why:** Photographers need complete archival backup while families should see only the photographer's deliberate selections. Using the visible star rating makes that selection fast and avoids a hidden second control.

**How to apply:** Keep backup independent from gallery eligibility. Synchronize portrait and group ratings after online or offline review, enforce the positive-rating condition in gallery lists, order validation, and protected media delivery, and never expose RAW files. Product preparation must use only the unique exact-5 sources.
