### Profiling result

The largest current bottleneck was **main-process Jimp thumbnail generation**:

| Strategy | JPEG avg | JPEG p50 | JPEG p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base64 thumbnail | 429 ms | 400 ms | 575 ms |
| Thumbnail written to disk | 400 ms | 397 ms | 428 ms |
| Direct local JPEG URL | **77 ms** | **77 ms** | **77 ms** |

For a 50-file burst:

- Base64 path: last preview at **16.3 s**, p50 **8.6 s**
